Virtual Physics Laboratory

Each experiment runs entirely in the browser and keeps your readings under your register number, so you can close the tab and come back. Every experiment ends with a report you can print, download or submit.

On a phone or tablet, use "Add to home screen" (or the Install button above) and the laboratory opens like an app and keeps working with no signal — everything but submission runs on the device.

Instructors: the admin console handles batches, students, assignments and submissions. the assessed link beside an experiment turns off the on-screen identification aids. Adding an experiment means one folder under experiments/ and one entry in data/catalog.js — see ADDING-AN-EXPERIMENT.md in the repository.
